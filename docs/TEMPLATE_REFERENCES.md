# Photographic template references

The supplied Dribbble pages and their linked design imagery were inspected, including the animation references' opening and final views. The layouts below adapt their composition to FolioNest's validated portfolio fields, rather than copying creator branding, photographs, unsupported claims or testimonials. Reference animations are adapted as restrained portrait/type entrances and photo hover effects. All effects respect reduced motion; image/PDF exports use a stable frame.

| Reference | Template | Adapted composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Alexfolio](https://dribbble.com/shots/26873011-Free-Alexfolio-Portfolio-template) | Monograph | Monochrome framed hero, oversized name and profile portrait; alternating project spreads. |
| [Personal Portfolio Website Animations](https://dribbble.com/shots/26995447-Personal-Portfolio-Website-Animations) | Portrait Motion | Outline and filled name typography, layered central portrait, spacious sections and gentle entrances. |
| [Personal Portfolio Website Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/24978876-Personal-Portfolio-Website-Design) | Quiet Atelier | Italic greeting, grayscale portrait column, quiet ruled sections and generous spacing. |
| [Videaste](https://dribbble.com/shots/23090247-Videaste-Personal-Videographer-Portfolio-Landing-Page-Website) | Cinematic | Cream, charcoal and gold; camera portrait, condensed name, rounded work and contrasting chapters. |
| [Carlos](https://dribbble.com/shots/10724776-Carlos-Personal-Portfolio-Website) | Ember | Charcoal canvas, warm accent, three-column introduction and asymmetric section cards. |
| [Personal Portfolio Website](https://dribbble.com/shots/24120347-Personal-Portfolio-Website) | Golden Hour | Warm glow, italic greeting, condensed name and editorial portrait composition. |

These designs share the real content renderer for editing, public HTML, gallery previews and exports. Normal uploaded photographs use a cover crop; transparent cutouts retain their full silhouette and align to the frame's bottom. Photos are optional. The gallery uses two fictional model identities, with female variants added at the user's request; uploaded owner photos remain independent of gallery samples.
